Euclid, Ohio

Euclid is on the southern shore of Lake Erie and has ZIP codes 44117, 44123 and 44132. Addresses in Euclid with ZIP code 44119 or 44143 are served too. Our whole line is based here, and every service on this site is available to Euclid homes and businesses.

Towns just outside our listed pages

Gates Mills, Mentor and Chesterland are close to our service area but do not have their own pages here. Call and tell dispatch your address. We will say plainly whether we can reach you and how the job would be handled.

How coverage works

Dispatch assigns crews by location and by what is already running. We do not promise a set arrival time, because traffic, weather and other emergencies change it. We do tell you what to do while you wait, and we tell you what we expect to find on arrival.

Wherever you are, the process is the same. We explain scope and price before work starts, remove the water, set drying equipment and keep readings.

One dispatch line for every city

Every city on this page reaches the same 24/7 number, (216) 541-8541. Dispatch asks for your address first and then the source of the water. That lets us route the right crew and tell you what to do before anyone arrives. We do not use a different line for each city, so there is nothing to look up in an emergency.

The four nearby cities each have a page with local notes. Wickliffe borders Euclid, Willowick and Willoughby. Eastlake sits on the Lake Erie shore. Willoughby has the Chagrin River running through it. Beachwood is inland and reached from Euclid by I-271 or State Route 175.

Permits and offices change by city

Repairs after a loss may need a permit, and each city has its own office. In Euclid, the Building and Housing Department is at 585 E. 222nd Street. Wickliffe, Eastlake and Willoughby are in Lake County, and Beachwood is in Cuyahoga County, so their rules and contacts differ. Ask your own city building department before you open walls or replace structural parts.

We can describe what drying and cleanup involve in any of these cities. The permit questions belong with the local office.

If your address is near a border

City limits do not always match the mailing address on your bill. A house with a Euclid mailing address may sit in a different municipality, and the reverse can be true. That does not slow the call. Give dispatch your street address and we will confirm that we can reach you.
