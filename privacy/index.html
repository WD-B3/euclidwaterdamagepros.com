Privacy Policy

What we collect, how we use it and what third-party services appear on this website.

Call (216) 541-8541

This policy explains how Euclid Water Damage Pros handles information when you visit this site, call our line or email us. It was last updated on November 9, 2026.

We keep this site simple. It has no contact forms, no user accounts and no checkout.

Information you give us

When you call or email, you may share your name, phone number, address and details about the damage. We use that information to respond to your request, schedule work, document the job and communicate with you about it.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. If you do not want a call recorded, tell the dispatcher at the start of the call.

Information collected by the website

Like most websites, this one is served from a host that may keep standard server logs such as IP address, browser type and pages requested. We use that kind of information to keep the site running and to understand problems.

Third-party services on this site

  • Google Maps embeds show our service area and city maps. Google may collect information when a map loads, according to its own policies.
  • Google Fonts delivers the typefaces. Google may receive your IP address when fonts load.

We do not control the practices of these services. Review their privacy policies for details.

How we share information

We share information only as needed to do the job, such as with subcontractors, your insurer when you ask us to, or as required by law. We do not sell personal information.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete information you gave us, subject to records we must keep. Email contact@euclidwaterdamagepros.com or call (216) 541-8541.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of the page.
