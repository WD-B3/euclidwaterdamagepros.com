Wickliffe from the Euclid line

Wickliffe is a city in western Lake County, bordered by Euclid, Willowick and Willoughby. Euclid Avenue runs through the corridor that connects our office to the city. Saint Mary Seminary stands at 28700 Euclid Avenue, a useful landmark if you are giving directions to a crew.

Before we arrive: if water is still spreading

Stop the source

Close the main water valve or the fixture valve. If the source is outside, move items away from the wall.

Power

Turn off power to wet areas only if the panel is dry and you can reach it. Otherwise call the utility.

Lift valuables

Raise furniture legs onto blocks and move paper, electronics and rugs to a dry room.

Keep clear

Keep children and pets away from standing water, especially if it came from a drain.

Call the line

Phone us 24/7 and describe the water. A dispatcher will route a crew.
