Flood cleanup and restoration for Euclid homes and businesses after rain, rising groundwater or overflow, handled around the clock.

Flood water is an emergency because it spreads fast and is rarely clean. Our dispatch line is open 24/7. Stay out of standing water, turn off power only if the panel is dry and reachable, and call the utility or 911 about any electrical or gas risk before you call us.

Flood damage cleanup in Euclid, OH is the removal of floodwater, silt and contaminated materials, followed by cleaning, disinfecting and drying. Flood water counts as Category 2 or Category 3 water under the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard, so porous materials that soaked it up usually cannot be saved.

When flood water is an emergency

Any water that came from outside, from a creek, from the ground or up through a drain is an emergency. It may carry sewage, fuel, fertilizer and bacteria, and it can energize a basement full of wiring. A few inches of such water is enough to ruin flooring and walls.

Stay out

Keep people and pets out of floodwater and do not wade in to rescue belongings.

Power

Leave the panel alone if it is wet. Call the electric utility or 911 for any electrical or gas risk.

Document

Photograph the water line on the walls before anything moves.

Call

Phone the 24/7 line and describe depth, source and what is floating.

Flood exposure around Euclid

Euclid lies along the southern shore of Lake Erie, and part of the Cleveland Metroparks Euclid Creek Reservation is inside the city. We do not state a flood zone for any street here. The FEMA Flood Map Service Center shows the mapped zone for your address, and the city Building and Housing Department at 585 E. 222nd Street can tell you what local rules apply to repairs.

Flood coverage matters too. Standard homeowners policies usually exclude flood damage, and a separate flood policy is the usual answer. Ask your insurer which water events your policy covers before you need it.

How flood cleanup works

Crews begin with safety. Standing water is pumped out, and mud and silt are shoveled and hauled away. Everything that soaked contaminated water is sorted into what can be cleaned and what must go.

  • Carpet, pad, insulation and soft furnishings that sat in flood water are usually discarded.
  • Drywall is cut out above the water line when readings show wicking.
  • Hard surfaces are cleaned and treated with an antimicrobial product.
  • Air movers and dehumidifiers dry what remains, with daily readings.

Rain, wind and the damage that follows

Rising water and rain through a damaged roof are different problems. Water that came in through the envelope, such as a roof, a window or a flashing, is covered on the storm water damage cleanup page. If flood water reached the lowest level, basement water damage cleanup covers contents and finished walls.

After the cleanup, structural drying and dehumidification brings framing and subfloor back to a normal range before rebuild.

Scope, price and your claim

Flood cleanup is priced by affected area, depth, how long the water sat and how many materials need removal. We explain the scope and the price before work begins. Never accept a number from a company that has not seen the water line.
