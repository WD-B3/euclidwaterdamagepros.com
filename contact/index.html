What to tell dispatch

  • Your address and the nearest cross street.
  • Where the water is coming from, and whether it is still running.
  • Which rooms are wet and how deep the water is.
  • Whether the water looks clean, gray or contaminated.
  • Whether anyone has turned off the main valve or the power.
  • The name of your insurer, if you want us to document for a claim.

Do this before you call

  1. Stay safe. Keep clear of wet areas where power is on.
  2. Close the main water valve if a pipe is the source.
  3. Move valuables to a dry place if it is safe.
  4. Take quick photos of the damage and the source.
  5. Call (216) 541-8541.
